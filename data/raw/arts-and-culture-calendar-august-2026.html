Arts and Culture Calendar (August 2026)

August 5, 2026
  • Return to News Page

Photo: “Enthused and Spiteful Hairpins” by Anthony Watson, 2025. Art in Public Places Collection of the State Foundation on Culture and the Arts. Guava, Hong Kong orchid, koa, bamboo, oyster shell, black coral, plastic, inorganic dye, 5160 steel, nylon. Purchased from the 2025-2026 “Oceans of Peace” exhibition at the Maui Arts and Cultural Center. On view in the “Arts of Oceania” mini-exhibit at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum August 7 – November 28, 2026. Free admission.

This arts and culture calendar features SFCA grantees, partners, and Hawaiʻi state and local government programs and events. A version of this calendar is also published in the SFCA email newsletter. Preview and subscribe online: SFCA August 2026 Arts and Culture Calendar email newsletter.

Questions regarding a particular program or event should be directed to the organization listed with the opportunity. Inclusion here should not be interpreted as an endorsement.

Family-friendly events

  • “Creative Micronesia” at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum. Events and mini-exhibit featuring Micronesian and Pacific Islands artists, activities, and performances. Learn more on the SFCA website: sfca.hawaii.gov/creative-micronesia-at-state-art-museum-exhibit-family-friendly-events-through-august-2026.
  • E Heluhelu Kākou: Swimming into Stories community event at Windward Community College (Kāneʻohe, Oʻahu). Saturday, August 1, 10:00 a.m. – noon. Storytelling, crafts, and hands-on ocean fun for kids ages 2-19. All ages welcome. For more information, please visit the Hale Laʻakea website: library.wcc.hawaii.edu/events/upcoming#s-lg-box-wrapper-41680612.
  • Hotel Street Art Hui mural painting and live music, Friday August 7, 4:00 – 6:00 p.m. at 29 N. Hotel Street (Honolulu Chinatown). Free, all ages welcome.
  • Mizu Wai / Story Weaving East Maui Waterways community story weaving project on Saturday August 8, 9:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. at Keʻanae Peninsula Ballpark (Maui). Learn more on the Nest Maker Space website: NestMakerspace.weebly.com/mizu-wai.html.
  • Drawn to Music: A  Collaborative Live Music and Art Event on Saturday, August 8 at Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Park (Chinatown, Honolulu), 1:00 – 4:00 p.m. For more information, visit the Guitar Foundation of Hawaiʻi website: GuitarFoundationHawaii.org.
  • Welcome Home Kōlea Festival at Ala Moana Beach Park (Honolulu) Saturday, September 19, 2026, 10:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. Event includes display of kōlea artwork. Learn more on the Hawaiʻi Audubon Society website: HiAudubon.org/events/welcome-home-kolea-2026.
  • Summer Flix free movie screening at the Prince Waikiki hotel. Wednesday 7:00 p.m. August 5, 2026. Get more information on the Hawaiʻi International Film Festival website: hiff.org/summer-flix.
  • Honolulu Museum of Art HoMA Nights, every Friday until 9:00 p.m. Live music and experiences, included with admission. For more information, please visit the Honolulu Museum of Art website: HonoluluMuseum.org/events.
  • ʻIolani Palace Kamaʻāina Sundays monthly event with free presentations and acitivities. For more information, please visit the ʻIolani Palace website: IolaniPalace.org/visit/kamaaina-Sundays.
  • Bishop Museum After Hours monthly event, 5:00 – 9:00 p.m. on the second Friday of each month, exploring Hawaiʻi’s cultural and natural history. For more information, please visit the Bishop Museum website: BishopMuseum.org/exhibits-and-programs/afterhours.
  • Hawaiʻi State Public Libraries offer free events including keiki storytime, crafting, games, and workshops. Explore the Hawaiʻi State Public Library System events: LibrariesHawaii.org/events.
  • First Friday at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum: free, all-ages of evening of art and music at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum every first Friday of the month, 5:00 – 9:00 p.m. First Fridays in Honolulu calendar: FirstFridayHawaii.com.
  • Super Saturday at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum, August 15, 11:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. Hands-on art making for all ages. The August Super Saturday features Micronesian and other Pacific Islander performances and activities.

Performances and Concerts

  • Drawn to Music: A  Collaborative Live Music and Art Event on  Saturday, August 8 at Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Park (Chinatown, Honolulu), 1:00 – 4:00  p.m. For more information, visit the Guitar Foundation of Hawaiʻi website: GuitarFoundationHawaii.org.
  • Diamond Head Theatre presents “Les Misérables” July 30 – August 16, 2026. More information on their website: DiamondHeadTheatre.com/shows-tickets.
  • Hawaiʻi Public Radio “Hawaiʻi Radio Hour” broadcasting from Kahilu Theatre, Sundays at 4:00 p.m. on HPR-1. Conversations, moʻolelo, and mele. Learn more and listen on the Kahilu website: kahilu.org/hawaii-radio-hour-episodes.
  • ʻIolani Palace Royal Hawaiian Band Performances on most Fridays, 12:00 – 1:00 p.m. Free outdoor concert, open to the public. Learn more on the ʻIolani Palace website: IolaniPalace.org/events/royal-hawaiian-band-concerts.
  • West Hawaiʻi County Band monthly concert at Hale Hālāwai County Park on Aliʻi Drive (Kailua-Kona, Hawaiʻi Island). Learn more about their monthly concerts on their website: WestHawaiiBand.com/schedule.

Exhibits

  • “Interwoven Views: threads of influence” exhibit of artworks by State of Hawaiʻi Department of Education art instructors. At Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum through August 26. On the ground floor in the cafe area. Learn more on the SFCA website: sfca.hawaii.gov/doe-art-teachers-exhibit-work-from-their-personal-art-practices-in-state-art-museum-through-august-26.
  • “Portal 3” at the Firehouse Gallery, Waimea Arts Council (Hawaiʻi Island). August 1 – September 27, 2026. Learn more on the Waimea Arts Council website: FirehouseArtGallery.org/category/in-the-firehouse-gallery. An SFCA Art Acquisition Selection Committee has visited this exhibit.
  • “LUMINOSITY: The Legacy” at Viewpoints Gallery Maui, August 2 – October 31, 2026. An SFCA Art Acquisition Selection Committee has visited this exhibit.
  • “Hawaiʻi’s Woodshow: Na Laʻau O Hawaiʻi”, August 7 – August 29, 2026 at the Downtown Art Center. Learn more on the Downtown Art Center website: DowntownArtHI.org/exhibitions. The SFCA Board of Commissioners has approved an Art Acquisition Selection Committee visit to this exhibit.
  • “16th Annual Abstract Only!” at Wailoa Center (Hawaiʻi Island), August 14 – September 17, 2026. Learn more on the Wailoa Center website: WailoaCenter.com/exhibitions-1. The SFCA Board of Commissioners has approved an Art Acquisition Selection Committee visit to this exhibit.
  • “ʻAimama” at Gallery ʻIolani, Windward Community College. August 27 – December 10, 2026. For more information, please visit the Gallery ʻIolani website: gallery.windward.hawaii.edu. The SFCA Board of Commissioners has approved an Art Acquisition Selection Committee visit to this exhibit.
  • “Trifecta” exhibit at Capitol Modern: the Hawaiʻi State Art Museum featuring artworks from the state collection, curated by members of the local arts community. Learn more on the Capitol Modern website: CapitolModern.org/exhibits/trifecta.
  • “Peace by Piece” quilt show at Volcano Art Center (Hawaiʻi Island), July 19 – August 11, 2026. Textile and Fabric Pop-Up July 25 and 26; Needlebook workshop August 2. Learn more on the Volcano Art Center website:  VolcanoArtCenter.org.
  • “2026 Pacific States Biennial North American” printmaking exhibition at the University of Hawaiʻi Hilo (Hawaiʻi Island) November 2, 2026 – January 14, 2027.
  • “Ea Mai ʻEiwa: Patterns of Practice” at the Bernice Pauahi Bishop Museum through September 20, 2026. Learn more on the museum website: BishopMuseum.org/Eiwa.
  • “AHA! Art Immersive” exhibit, July 3 – August 6, 2026 at Wailoa Center (Hilo, Hawaiʻi Island). More information on the Wailoa Center website: WailoaCenter.com/exhibitions-1. An SFCA Art Acquisition Selection Committee has visited this exhibit.
  • University of Hawaiʻi Insect Museum art show at Honolulu Printmakers, opening reception October 24, 2026. More information to come on the UH Insect Museum website: ctahr.hawaii.edu/insectmuseum.

About the State Foundation on Culture and the Arts

The Hawai‘i State Foundation on Culture and the Arts (SFCA), Hawaiʻi’s state government agency, was established by the Hawai‘i State Legislature in 1965 to promote, perpetuate, preserve and encourage culture and the arts, history and the humanities as central to the quality of life of the people of Hawai‘i. SFCA funding is provided by the state of Hawai‘i and the National Endowment for the Arts. SFCA is attached to the State of Hawaiʻi Department of Business, Economic Development, and Tourism (DBEDT).
